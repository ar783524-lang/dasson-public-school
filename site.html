About Us

Rooted in the hills of Rajgarh, built for the whole child.

Dasson Public High School brings together strong academics, character, and a sense of place. Set against the mountains of Rajgarh, we give students room to think clearly, work hard, and grow into confident, capable people.

  • Balanced academics from primary through senior secondary
  • Small class sizes with individual attention
  • Sports, arts, and co-curricular activities woven into every week
  • A safe, close-knit campus community

Admission Overview

Admissions at Dasson Public High School run through the academic year, subject to seat availability in each class. We welcome children from Nursery through Class 10 and keep the process simple and transparent, so parents know exactly what to expect at every step — from the first enquiry to the day your child joins us.
